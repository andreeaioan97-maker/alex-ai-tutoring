Not at all. Alex works with complete beginners and experienced professionals alike, and starts wherever you are.

What do we actually do in a session?

We start from something you want to get done (writing, planning, research, an idea) and work on it together, live, using real AI tools.

Yes. Every plan starts with a short chat about your goals, and Custom Support Plans are shaped entirely around you.

Which languages are sessions in?

English or Romanian, whichever you are more comfortable with.

Many AI tools have free versions. Alex will help you work out what you actually need before you spend anything.
